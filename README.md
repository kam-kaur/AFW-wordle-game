# AFW

A Wordle-inspired word game I built with a team during Dev Academy Aotearoa. The word length changes from round to round, there's no limit on guesses, and your score goes on a leaderboard.

## My role

On the technical side, I contributed to frontend components, API requests, game logic, client-side testing, and interactive features such as sound effects and the Give Up functionality. I also shaped the visual side of the game, working on the styling, colours, themes and typography to create its overall look and feel. I worked with Git and GitHub throughout the project, including branching, pull requests and integrating changes with the team.

I was also our official **Vibes Watcher**, a role focused on keeping the team connected, supported and enjoying the process. I led stand-ups and retrospectives, contributed to planning, helped keep communication flowing, and brought a bit of fun and energy when things got challenging.

## What I learned

Working in a team is hard until you find a way of working that suits everyone. We had conflicting ideas early on about how the game should look and work, and it took a few honest conversations to sort out. What helped was keeping our retros small and stress-free so they never felt like a massive thing, and taking breaks to play games so we stayed connected. Once we had a system that worked, the project went from stressful to something I really enjoyed.

## Features

* Guess words and get feedback on each letter
* Play rounds with words of different lengths
* Keep guessing until you solve the word or give up
* Track your scores and game results
* View the leaderboard and compare scores
* Create an account and keep your games connected to you
* Enjoy sound effects and other interactive touches
